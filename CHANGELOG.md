## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Places of power from OpenStreetMap: houses of worship of every faith, wayside chapels, springs, trees, stones, heights; "here" by each place's radius.
- The sitting: ten minutes with the screen off, kept honest by a foreground service watching the screen, the place, the phone's motion and faked locations; two soft pulses at ten minutes, and you may stay.
- Stamps inked in code: a frame per place, a silhouette per kind, ink by the sun, rain, frost, wind and fog on the paper, rings for twenty and forty minutes.
- Two words after each sitting, notes in the crevice that open on another day, and how to come in for each kind of place.
- Places of your own in pencil, inked on the third day, with an optional anonymous note for the OSM mappers; tying a misplaced map point to where it really is.
- A weekly rumour of one place nearby, shown on the map as a vague circle; home found by nights.
- The book: notebooks per town, walks of three places or more, the year's circle with a name of your own, and the year's scroll.
- EN and RU; demo build; community links in settings.
